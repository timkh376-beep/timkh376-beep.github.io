See the menu

The idea

Three good things, done slowly

Coffee in the morning, a proper kitchen through the day, and a cellar that takes over at dusk. Most places pick one. We never saw the point.

The coffee is roasted dark and pulled short. The kitchen cooks from a menu that runs from beef tartare to a good cheeseburger without apologising for either. The wine list is small on purpose — eleven bottles from Bordeaux and Württemberg, all by the glass, so you can be curious without committing to the whole evening.

Nothing here is designed to be finished quickly. Tables are held for the length of the conversation, not the length of the meal.

Wine days

A different glass every day

One bottle takes the glass each day, and the kitchen sends the plate that goes with it. Marked evenings are tastings — five glasses, a long table, and a price on the door.
